## Decisions and notes

- **Auth `amr` facts (measured on Auth 2.197.0):** invite, magic-link, signup and recovery link verifications all
  produce `amr = [{"method":"otp", …}]`; password sign-in produces `password`. The database therefore accepts the
  email-link family (`otp` plus the more specific names in case Auth starts emitting them) and nothing else. SMS OTP
  would also be `otp`, hence the hosted requirement "phone sign-in OFF".
- **UX consequence of M2:** signing in with a password and then opening an invitation shows the uniform "not
  available" page; the emailed link (new account: invite link; existing account: magic link) is the way in. The T11
  e2e already follows the email link, so no UI change was needed.
- **e2e adaptation (`apps/web/e2e/support/supabase.ts`, minimal, forced by `enable_confirmations = true`):**
  `createDisposableUser` now follows the real confirmation link from Mailpit after `signUp`. Playwright runs with one
  worker, so `clearMailbox` in `auth.spec.ts` cannot race it.
- **Coordinator-approved changes outside the original ownership** (after T13 finished): merged `main` (T13) into
  this branch, resolving `local-demo.mjs` in favour of T13's shared env preparation; removed the now unused
  `DOMUS_INVITATION_TOKEN_KEY` from `scripts/prepare-function-env.mjs`; added the probe step to `.github/workflows/ci.yml`.
- `KnowledgeBase/GLOSSARY.md`: added `invitation_delivered_at` and `support_session_revocation_reason`, and restored
  the accents that had been saved as `?` in the M01 rows.
- Local stack: restarted once (`supabase stop/start`) to apply the Auth config, reset several times, functions served
  from this worktree for probes and e2e (all approved by the coordinator).

## Open questions / follow-ups

- **Spec decision (L3):** may a tenant administrator with `access_audit` (who sees support sessions per D18) also
  revoke them? Not implemented; only MFA platform administrators can.
- **Hosted (D19):** apply the "Hosted configuration" table of `M01-db.md` (confirm email ON, autoconfirm OFF, phone
  sign-in OFF, secure password change recommended) and delete the obsolete `DOMUS_INVITATION_TOKEN_KEY` secret.
- Concurrency of H1 is enforced by row locks (`FOR UPDATE` on the person at creation and acceptance, on the
  invitation by the gate) plus the per-user advisory lock; pgTAP is single-session, so the race itself is covered by
  the "already bound" and "competing invitation" behaviours rather than by a parallel test.
